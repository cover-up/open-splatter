# Living Backdrop

Add `SplatterBackdrop` to any GameObject in a scene and press play. It builds its own full-screen canvas, paints a composition one splat at a time, then keeps adding splats and dissolving the oldest. Click to punch holes in the paint. The inspector exposes the seed, the palette, the timing, the paint gain and the outer glow (above 0 each canvas is shown through a `SplatGlow` of its own).
